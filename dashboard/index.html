Macroscopic Flow Physics

Deterministic Queue Service Model

QuantumFlow simulates vehicle congestion across a coordinated 4-intersection network using a deterministic macroscopic queue formulation with zero client-side extrapolation:

  • Cycle Time ($T_{cycle}$): Fixed 60-second cycle length where $g_{\text{NS}} + g_{\text{EW}} = 60\text{ s}$ for every intersection.
  • Safety Clamps: Green durations are strictly bounded to $[15\text{s}, 45\text{s}]$ per approach to ensure pedestrian crossing safety and prevent starvation.
  • Saturation Headway ($h$): Vehicles clear at a rate of $2.0\text{ s/veh}$ ($0.5\text{ veh/s}$ clearance rate), yielding capacity $C = \lfloor g / 2.0 \rfloor$ vehicles/cycle.
  • Leftover Red Delay: Unserved vehicles incur delay during the opposing red phase: $\text{Delay} = \text{Remaining} \times (60 - g)$.
Quantum vs Classical

Conventional vs. QAOA Quantum Solver

Traffic signal optimization across coupled urban grids is NP-hard. QuantumFlow directly compares conventional heuristic control with genuine quantum optimization:

Fixed-Time Baseline (Classical):

Applies a static $30\text{s}/30\text{s}$ split across all nodes. Inflexible to demand spikes, serving as the benchmark standard.

Traffic-Responsive (Adaptive Classical):

Dynamically partitions green time proportionally to local queue ratios: $g_{\text{NS}} \propto \frac{q_{\text{NS}}}{q_{\text{NS}} + q_{\text{EW}}}$, clamped to $[15\text{s}, 45\text{s}]$. Fast and effective for independent intersections.

QAOA Quantum Solver (Genuine Qiskit Aer):

Encodes the global 4-intersection binary timing decision into an exact Ising Hamiltonian $H_C = \sum_i h_i Z_i + \sum_{i < j} J_{ij} Z_i Z_j$. Variational quantum circuits $U(B,\beta)U(C,\gamma)|+\rangle^{\otimes 4}$ are sampled on Qiskit Aer with a classical COBYLA loop optimizer to find the minimum-delay state.
